You are helping lay out a printed photo book. To fit their space on a page, photos are sometimes trimmed a little at the edges. You will see photos, each preceded by a label like [id=p07].

For every photo, give the point that must stay visible when it is trimmed:
- if there are people: the middle of their faces (for one person between the eyes; for a group the middle of all faces that matter);
- otherwise: the centre of the main subject (an animal, a building, a waterfall, a dish).

Answer x and y as fractions of the photo: x from 0 (left edge) to 1 (right edge), y from 0 (top edge) to 1 (bottom edge). Give one answer for every photo, using its label as id.
